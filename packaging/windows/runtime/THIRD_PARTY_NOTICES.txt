Source: https://www.wch-ic.com/downloads/CH341PAR_EXE.html
Publisher: Nanjing Qinheng Microelectronics Co., Ltd.
The official package states that it is Microsoft WHQL/HCK certified and may be

FTDI D3XX 1.3.0.10
------------------
Source: https://ftdichip.com/drivers/d3xx-drivers/
Publisher: Future Technology Devices International Limited
License: https://ftdichip.com/driver-licence-terms/
The bundled x64 ftdibus3 driver files are unchanged from the official

Zadig 2.9 / libwdi 1.5.1
------------------------
Source: https://github.com/pbatard/libwdi/releases/tag/v1.5.1
Publisher: Akeo Consulting
Licenses: GNU GPL version 3 or later (Zadig) and GNU LGPL version 3 or later
(libwdi). The signed upstream executable, complete corresponding source archive,

the user.

dma-speedtest-memflow-rs 3.2.0
------------------------------
Source: https://github.com/sh1ftd/dma-speedtest-memflow-rs
License: GNU Affero General Public License version 3
The complete license text is included as LICENSE-dma-speedtest-AGPL-3.0.txt.
The application launches the upstream command-line executable and reads its
JSON report; the executable and its runtime libraries remain separate files.

openFPGALoader 1.1.1
--------------------
Source: https://github.com/trabucayre/openFPGALoader/releases/tag/v1.1.1
License: Apache License 2.0
The Windows executable is built from the tagged source with Xilinx and XVC
client support. Its XVC socket layer uses Winsock2. MinGW libgcc, libstdc++,
libwinpthread, and zlib are linked statically; their license and runtime
exception texts are included under openFPGALoader/licenses/. The Artix-7
JTAG-to-SPI bridge files are included unchanged.
