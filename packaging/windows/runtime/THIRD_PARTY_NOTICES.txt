openFPGALoader 1.1.1
--------------------
Source: https://github.com/trabucayre/openFPGALoader/releases/tag/v1.1.1
License: Apache License 2.0
The XVC Windows executable is built from the tagged source with Xilinx and XVC
client support. Its XVC socket layer uses Winsock2. The separate FTDI executable
uses the reproducible patch at packaging/windows/openfpgaloader-ftdi.patch. That
patch enables dynamic libusb 1.0.27 and libftdi 1.5 runtimes and adds the RS DMA
Interface A mapping (data 0x98, direction 0x8B). MinGW libgcc, libstdc++,
libwinpthread, and zlib are linked statically; their license and runtime
exception texts are included under openFPGALoader/licenses/. The Artix-7
JTAG-to-SPI bridge files are included unchanged.
